How Prolance Works

A three-stage contract lifecycle engineered for certainty and quality delivery.

01.

Scope & Publish

Clients define project constraints, required tech stacks, deadlines, and minimum/maximum budget expectations.

OUTPUT: Public Structured Listing
02.

Bid & Atomic Hire

Freelancers propose custom delivery fees and timetables. Clients review candidate portfolios and hire via atomic database transactions.

OUTPUT: Single Guaranteed Assignment
03.

Deliver & Review

Participants cooperate in dedicated project rooms. Deliverables are approved, status is finalized, and peer reviews reflect on public profiles.

OUTPUT: Verified Completed Contract
