Workflow Specification

The Prolance Operating Model

A transparent, deterministic methodology designed to replace bureaucratic freelancing platforms with direct engineering cooperation.

C

The Client Playbook

Hiring and delivery lifecycle

01. Define Constraints

Publish Structured Specs

Provide unambiguous deliverables, tech stack requirements, fixed or hourly budget limits, and deadlines.

02. Evaluate Candidates

Atomic Decisioning

Compare proposed fees and timetables side-by-side. Accepting a candidate automatically reserves the contract and notifies applicants.

03. Review & Sign Off

Direct Milestone Delivery

Test submitted repositories or artifacts inside your shared workspace. Request revisions or approve completion to finalize peer reviews.

F

The Freelancer Playbook

Proposal and reputation lifecycle

01. Curate Profile

Showcase Proven Artifacts

Set your transparent hourly rate, link live GitHub demos and design cases, and state your availability.

02. High-Conviction Bids

Detailed Implementation Plans

Submit proposals with clear scope breakdown and timelines. Edit or withdraw proposals dynamically while listings remain open.

03. Deliver & Accumulate Proof

Build Unfakeable Reputation

Upload deliverables directly to the client channel. Earn 1-5 star peer ratings that permanently boost your marketplace ranking.
